Privacy Policy

Letta · Effective August 19, 2026

Letta is a one-way messaging app operated by Stampede Ventures. This policy describes what we collect, why, and what we never do.

What we collect

  • Phone number. Your phone number is your account. We use it to sign you in (via SMS code) and so people who already know your number can send you messages.
  • Display name. The name you choose, shown to people you message.
  • Messages. The messages you send are stored so recipients can read them. Letta is one-way: recipients can never reply, react, or see any status.
  • Device push token. If you allow notifications, we store an Apple push token to tell your device when a message arrives.

Contacts

If you use the contact picker, only the single contact you tap is shared with the app — Letta never receives your address book. If you grant Contacts access to find friends, phone numbers are sent to our server only to check for a match and are not stored. Your contacts are never uploaded, retained, or used for anything else.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no trackers. The app contains no ad SDKs or analytics SDKs.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We never use your data to train AI models.
  • Anonymous messages are delivered without the sender's identity, and it is never revealed to the recipient.

Service providers

Messages and account data are stored with Supabase (our database host). Push notifications are delivered through Apple's push service. SMS sign-in codes are delivered via standard SMS. These providers process data only to provide the service.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app menu (Compose → ··· → Delete account). Deletion permanently erases your profile, phone number, push tokens, and every message you've sent — including from recipients' inboxes. You can also request deletion by emailing gregsilverman1@me.com from any address, including the phone number on the account.

Children

Letta is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
