Letta

One-way messaging. Say it once. No reply needed.

What is Letta?

Letta is a one-way messaging app. You send a message; the recipient can read it but can never reply, react, or see any status. No read receipts, no typing indicators, no conversations — just the message.

Support

Account deletion

You can delete your account from inside the app, or email gregsilverman1@me.com from any address and include the phone number on the account. Deletion removes your profile, device tokens, and undelivered messages.
